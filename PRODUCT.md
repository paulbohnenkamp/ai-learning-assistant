# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Software-development learners and trainers using a focused local-first chat tool
for practice, explanations, quizzes, and troubleshooting.

## Product Purpose

DevCoach AI helps users build full-stack engineering confidence through concise,
reviewable guidance across Java, Spring Boot, Node.js, React, microservices, and
AI-augmented development.

## Brand Personality

Confident, practical, encouraging. The interface should feel focused and
capable, with enough energy to make practice inviting without distracting from
the conversation.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy dashboard chrome, decorative motion without purpose, low-contrast
text, and a generic “AI chat” appearance that makes the learning workflow feel
like a demo instead of a useful tool.

## Design Principles

- Keep the learner’s question and the assistant’s guidance central.
- Make technical answers easy to scan and act on.
- Show expert confidence without making learners feel judged.
- Prefer clear feedback and accessible defaults over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong color contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, readable text sizing,
visible focus states, semantic structure, and reduced-motion-friendly behavior.
